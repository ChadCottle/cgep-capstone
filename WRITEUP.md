### Framework Choice   

I am opting to go with SOC2 Type II to demonstrate that my controls are not just a point-in-time screenshot.  
These controls work over time and demonstrate a repeatable process that is in line with the Trust Services Criteria.  

There are arguments to be made that SOC2 Type II is a sales enabler and "commercial trust signal" per Drata, but its flexibility allows us to define how ACME Health meets the Trust Services Criteria.  

Many of ACME Health's potential (and current) customers ask us for a copy of the SOC 2 Type II under NDA or a SOC 3. As we are young and eager to grow company, this feels like the right framework as it addresses aligns with the company's plan to generate revenue and customers and ensures that security is not an afterthought or ad hoc process. The goal is to bring rigor to engineering and security while providing an opportunity to positively impact ACME's sales/revenue generating capabilities.
