### Framework Choice   

As the GRC Engineer for ACME Heath, I am suggesting that senior management focuses on SOC 2 Type II. Why?  SOC2 Type II to demonstrates that controls are not just a point-in-time screenshot.  

These controls work over time and demonstrate a repeatable process that is in line with the Trust Services Criteria.  

There are arguments to be made that SOC2 Type II is more of  a sales enabler and "commercial trust signal,", but its flexibility allows us to define how ACME Health's controls  meet the Trust Services Criteria.  

Many of ACME Health's potential (and current) customers have asked us for a copy of the SOC 2 Type II (under NDA) or a SOC 3. As we are young and eager-to-grow company, this feels like the right framework as it aligns with the company's plan to generate revenue, acquire new customers and ensures that security is not an afterthought or ad hoc process. The goal is to bring rigor to engineering and security while providing an opportunity to positively impact ACME's sales/revenue generating capabilities.
